                    NFL DFS DraftKings & FanDuel Expert Survey: Wednesday Night Football Picks

                    DraftKings

                    Who are your three favorite Captains, ranked in order?

                    headChopper: Kyren Williams, Matthew Golden, Christian Watson
                    ncorfield8k: Kyren Williams, Blake Corum, Matthew Golden
                    Davis Mattek: Matthew Golden, Kyren Williams, Christian Watson

                    How do you plan to beat the field of opponents in the tournaments you will be playing? What tactic or strategy will set you apart?

                    headChopper: I’ll lean toward receivers at Captain over the quarterbacks, with Matthew Golden and Christian Watson offering some of the highest ceilings in the game at lower Captain pOWN%. The matchup I really want to lean into is the Rams’ backfield. Green Bay ranks 24th in schedule-adjusted fantasy points allowed to running backs, and the Rams are 2.5-point favorites, so Kyren Williams is my top Captain and MVP play, with Blake Corum as a likely low-owned pivot if the Rams take control on the ground. I’ll also be pairing the Rams D/ST with Williams since their defense ranks in the top 6 in fantasy points allowed to quarterbacks, running backs, and wide receivers.

                    ncorfield8k: I’ll play a lot of the Rams running backs, and I’ll play a lot of the Rams defense. After a slow start to the season, the Rams defense has been dominant lately. They’ve allowed just 28 total points over their last 3 games. The return of Myles Garrett for the last 2 helped. It’s a much tougher matchup with the Packers, but the Packers also gave up 8 sacks to the Vikings in their last game. Their offensive line could get to starters back, but they were already subpar, so I like the Rams’ odds of causing some chaos again.

                    Davis Mattek: The Rams played the most 2-RB personnel of any team in the NFL in Week 10 against the Cardinals. I expect that is going to be their plan heading into this game with no Puka Nacua, Davante Adams, and Terrance Ferguson. Blake Corum and Kyren Williams can and should be played together in lineups way over the field average.

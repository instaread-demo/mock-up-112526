J. Cole is stopping by Ms Collins after his sold-out show to host a party

words by staff writer

Melbourne's night owls have been handed a good reason to skip bedtime, because J. Cole is hosting an afterparty.

J. Cole will host The Official Fall-Off Tour Afterparty at Ms Collins on 29 November, kicking off straight after his sold-out concert.

Ms Collins, on Collins Street, is the prime spot for the after-hours session, giving concertgoers somewhere to head once the main show wraps.

Doors open at 9pm and the night rolls on until 5am, which leaves plenty of time to catch your breath, find a drink and keep the evening going. The Grammy Award-winning rapper is billed as both hosting and making a special appearance, so guests can expect to share a room with him.

  • When: 29 November, doors 9pm until 5am
  • Age: 18+, valid ID required

TISM headline a free all-ages Fed Square party for Triple R’s 50th birthday

TISM
words by staff writer

TISM are slipping on their balaclavas for Fed Square presents: 50 Years of Triple R, a free all-ages gig.

Fed Square presents: 50 Years of Triple R is round two of the station’s birthday celebrations at the CBD venue, and it’s the last public party of the milestone year.

Triple R has been broadcasting across Melbourne since 1976 and is wrapping up its 50th year with this one. Station manager Dave Houchin has described the night as a thank you to the volunteers and subscribers who have kept the not-for-profit station on air.

Fed Square is presenting and producing the event as part of its push to make live music free for Victorians. Fed Square Director and CEO Katrina Sedgwick says the venue wants to keep backing Melbourne music.

Fed Square presents: 50 Years of Triple R

  • Where: Fed Square Main Stage
  • When: 28 November, from 6.15pm
  • Who: TISM, The Bamboos (with special guests Tim Rogers + WILSN) and Automatic (US)
  • Presented by: Fed Square and Triple R
  • Cost: Free
  • Ages: All ages
  • Note: Parents and guardians, please be advised that this event may contain explicit language and adult themes.

Stay up to date with what’s happening in and around Melbourne here. 

  

Triple R reaches an audience of more than one million people. It counts 17,000 subscribers and donors and relies on 695 volunteers. The station is known for championing local music and art, covering topics that don’t get much airtime elsewhere, and giving aspiring broadcasters a place to learn the ropes.

Triple R MCs and DJs will also be part of the night.

The lineup starts with TISM, the entirely anonymous, balaclava-wearing band that emerged in the late 80s. They’ve spent decades mixing dance, pop and rock with plenty of sarcasm, and their live shows are known for being unpredictable.

The Bamboos are now in their 26th year as funk and soul heavyweights. Their 12th studio album, Sugar on Top, came out in 2026, and the nine-piece band is fronted by Kylie Auldist and led by Lance Ferguson’s production.

Tim Rogers and WILSN will join them on the night. Over the years the band have teamed up with vocalists including Aloe Blacc, Megan Washington and Alice Russell.

Rounding things out is Automatic from Los Angeles, who have spent nearly a decade making post-punk, new wave and deviant pop on Stones Throw Records. Their third album is called Is It Now?
